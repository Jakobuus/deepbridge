# Feature - 3,4,3-LI(1,2-HOPO) an orally active actinide decor

3,4,3-LI(1,2-HOPO): an orally active actinide decorporation agent (plutonium, uranium, americium, neptunium), preclinical, well tolerated in rats at high oral doses
