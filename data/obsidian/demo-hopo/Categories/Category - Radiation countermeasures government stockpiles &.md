Radiation countermeasures: government stockpiles & defense

The founder's papers report that 3,4,3-LI(1,2-HOPO) showed much higher actinide-removal efficacy than DTPA in preclinical work and was well tolerated orally in rats.
- Problem: Treating internalized plutonium, uranium and other actinides after a radiological or nuclear incident
- Problem: Current therapies for radionuclide contamination are limited
- Problem: Oral options for emergency medical use
- Problem: DTPA is approved for intravenous use only (nebulised: adults only)
- Problem: Stockpiling countermeasures for radiological and nuclear threats
